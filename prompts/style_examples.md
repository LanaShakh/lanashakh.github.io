## Observed Style Notes (from existing articles)

Based on the its-ai.org/blog articles by Svetlana Shakhova:

- **Tone:** Professional but accessible. Expert without being academic.
- **Article length:** 9-18 min reads (roughly 2000-4000 words)
- **Structure:** Clear H2 sections, logical flow, FAQ sections
- **Audience:** Teachers, writers, business professionals, students
- **Language:** EN (primary blog language, also available in RU)
- **Topics:** AI detection tools, benchmarks (MGTD), practical guides for professionals
- **Citation style:** References to benchmarks, research papers, official tool documentation

## Voice Keywords
Use these words to describe the target voice when setting up AI agents:
- Knowledgeable but not condescending
- Data-driven (specific numbers, benchmarks, comparisons)
- Practical (actionable takeaways, not just theory)
- Direct (gets to the point, respects reader's time)
