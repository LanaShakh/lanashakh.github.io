An anniversary gift for your parents: a countryside break for two

Blog

Give your parents a few days away together for their wedding anniversary. Time to walk, enjoy dinner, wake up to a beautiful view and simply be with each other. You can choose the cabin, make the booking and arrange a few extras, then present the whole trip as a surprise.

At Na Krayu Zemli, a glamping retreat near Kozelsk, they will have a private chalet surrounded by woods and fields. You can add an aromatic outdoor soak and a chef-prepared dinner. It is a gift that shows you have thought about them: where they would like to stay, what they enjoy and how they might spend a few days together.

The gift begins before the trip

When you give them the present, show them a glimpse of what is waiting: a cabin with a large window, a terrace among the trees, a valley in the evening light. Put a photograph of the chalet inside a card and add a few personal words from the family.

If you know when they are free, you can have the trip booked before the celebration. If their plans are still uncertain, give them the invitation and choose the dates together. The place and the little extras will still be a surprise.

You do not have to reveal everything at once. Your parents might know where they are going without knowing about the chef-prepared dinner or the aromatic hot tub soak. There will be more to discover after they arrive.

A chalet that feels right for them

The panoramic chalets look out over the Seryona Valley: fields, groves and distant woodland. If your parents enjoy sitting with a cup of tea and watching the landscape, the view becomes part of the gift. It will be there at breakfast, while they read by the window and during an evening on the terrace.

The cabins among the trees have a different feel. Trunks and branches are closer, with details of the woodland just outside the window. It is a comfortable place to settle down after a walk, talk and make plans for the next day.

Inside, the bed is made and there is a private bathroom and kitchen. Think about your parents' preferences: the sleeping arrangements they find comfortable, whether they would enjoy a fireplace or a larger terrace. The chalets have different layouts, bed arrangements and hot tub locations, which you can compare before choosing.

A few thoughtful extras from you

You can add the things your parents particularly enjoy. For some, that will be a good dinner; for others, an evening in a hot tub under the open sky. You can choose both and leave them free to decide how to spend the day.

An evening scented with cedar or spices

Picture your parents stepping out of their chalet and into warm water. Steam rises into the cool evening air. Beyond the tub are the valley or the trees beside their cabin. They can sit together, talk and watch the woodland gradually darken.

An aromatic soak adds another detail to the evening. The cedar option has a fresh conifer scent, while Mulled Wine brings citrus and spices. It is a lovely extra to choose in secret when you know which scents they enjoy. See the options on the hot tub page.

Dinner chosen around their tastes

For a celebratory dinner, you could choose grilled trout with pasta in a creamy champagne sauce. Or the signature barbecue dinner: chicken, halloumi and vegetables with a sauce of sour cream, garlic, paprika and herbs. The chef takes care of the cooking while your parents enjoy an evening at the table together.

You can think about the following morning too. Breakfast options include syrniki, traditional cottage cheese pancakes, with sour cream and blueberries, crepes with cottage cheese, and bread with farmhouse butter. Include something they love, perhaps the dish you would order for them at a family meal.

The menu will help you choose. You can agree on the order and serving time before the trip. If your parents enjoy cooking outdoors themselves, the chalet has a barbecue area, so making their favourite dishes can be part of their own plans for the evening.

You arrange the gift; they choose their day

Once you have given them the present, you can take care of the preparations and leave the weekend itself to them.

They might want a slow breakfast and a walk near the chalet. Or a trip into Kozelsk, a look around the fairytale buildings of Vikhlyandiya, or a pottery workshop. You know what interests them, so you could include a few ideas with the invitation:

  • What to see in Kozelsk — for parents who enjoy small towns and walks.
  • Vikhlyandiya Park — for unusual places and photographs.
  • Activities at the glamping retreat — for something new to try during their stay.

This little collection of ideas can be another part of the surprise. Your parents can look through the photographs and choose what appeals to them. For ideas for the anniversary itself, see A glamping anniversary: ideas for a romantic weekend.

The gift of being looked after

When children arrange a trip for their parents, the pleasure is not only in the place. It is also in knowing that someone has thought about the journey, chosen a cabin and organised dinner.

You could drive them there, help with their bags and give them a hug before leaving. Or send them the address, route and booking confirmation if they prefer to travel in their own car.

It helps to put the practical details in the invitation: dates, the chosen chalet, prepaid extras and a contact number. Your parents will know what you have arranged, while you can keep track of the booking details.

If you can arrange two nights, they will have a whole day between arrival and departure. Time for morning tea, a walk and an evening spent exactly as they please.

An invitation to their own little trip

Leave room in the card beside their wedding photograph for a new picture from the trip. Add a few words from their children about what you are grateful for and why you wanted to give them these days away.

Celebrate with the whole family, then let the occasion continue with just the two of them. Wish them a good journey, give them a hug and send them off for a few days you have already taken care of.

Questions about giving your parents a trip

Can I give them a trip before we know the dates?

You can give your parents a card inviting them for a break, then choose the dates together after the celebration. Make the booking separately once you have agreed on the dates and chalet.

How do I book if I am paying but my parents will be staying?

When booking, explain that the trip is a gift for your parents. The team will tell you which guest details they need. You can also agree on who should receive the confirmation and who will discuss payment and any extras.

Can the gift include accommodation, a hot tub and meals?

You can order a hot tub, aromatic additions and chef-prepared dishes separately alongside the stay. Agree on what is included and the total price in advance, so you know exactly what will be ready for your parents.

What if I want to keep some of the gift a secret?

When arranging the trip, tell the team which details you would like to keep as a surprise. Your parents could know about the chalet booking but discover the dinner or the aromatic soak during their stay. Agree on what can be arranged in advance.

How can I arrange the journey if my parents do not drive?

You can drive them yourself or plan public transport to Kozelsk, followed by a taxi to the glamping retreat in Yudinki. The return journey can be part of the gift too.
