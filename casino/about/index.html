What CasinoScope is

CasinoScope is a specialist online casino review publication. We do not list unlicensed or offshore casinos.

Editorial policy

Our reviews are independent. We test casinos with our own money and report what actually happens — real payout times, real bonus terms — not operator marketing. Every review is written by a named analyst and checked by an editor before publication.

  • Independence: ratings are based only on testing and verifiable facts.
  • Transparency: we disclose that we may earn commissions from sign-up links; this never changes a score.
  • Accuracy: licence numbers and key facts are verified against primary sources and dated.
  • Corrections: if we get something wrong, we fix it and note the update. Contact us to flag an error.
  • Safer gambling: every review carries 18+ and responsible-gambling guidance.
