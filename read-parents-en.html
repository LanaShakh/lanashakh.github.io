A wedding anniversary gift for your parents: a countryside break for two

Published original · RUBack to portfolio

Give your parents a few days away together for their wedding anniversary. Time to walk, share a meal, wake up to a lovely view and enjoy each other’s company. You can take care of choosing the cabin, making the booking and adding a few thoughtful extras — then present the trip as a surprise.

At Na Krayu Zemli, a glamping retreat near Kozelsk, they will have a private chalet among woodland and open fields. Add an aromatic hot tub bath and dinner prepared by a chef. It is a gift that shows the care you have put into it: you chose the setting, thought about their tastes and arranged a few days just for them.

Choose a chalet for your parents or discuss your gift with the team.

The gift begins before the journey

When you congratulate them, you can already give your parents a glimpse of the stay: a cabin with a large window, a terrace among the trees, the valley in the evening light. Put a photograph of the chalet in their card and add a few personal words from the family.

If you know which dates work for them, you can book the trip before presenting it. If they have their own plans, give them the invitation and choose the dates together afterwards. The destination and the details of the stay can still be a surprise.

You do not have to reveal everything at once. Your parents may know where they are going without knowing about the chef’s dinner or the aromatic bath you have chosen. The gift can continue to unfold after they arrive.

A chalet that suits them

Our panoramic chalets overlook the Seryona valley: fields, groves and woodland in the distance. If your parents enjoy sitting with a cup of tea and watching the landscape, the view becomes part of the gift. It will be there over breakfast, while they read by the window and during an evening on the terrace.

The woodland chalets have a different atmosphere. Tree trunks and branches are closer, and the forest fills the window. They are a lovely place to settle in after a walk, talk and make plans for the next day.

Inside, there is a freshly made bed, a private bathroom and a kitchen. Think about your parents’ habits as you choose: where they would be comfortable sleeping, whether they would enjoy a fireplace, and how much terrace space they would like. The chalets differ in layout, sleeping arrangements and the position of the hot tub. You can compare those details in the listings.

Browse the chalet catalogue to find a place you would love to give them.

A few thoughtful extras from you

Add something your parents particularly enjoy. For some, the highlight will be a good dinner. For others, an evening in an outdoor hot tub. You can choose both and let them decide how to spend the day.

An evening scented with cedar or spices

Picture your parents stepping out of the chalet and into warm water. Steam rises around them, while the cool evening air touches their faces. In front of them are the valley or the trees beside the cabin. They can sit together, talk and watch the woodland slowly darken.

An aromatic bath adds another layer to the evening. The cedar option has a woodland scent; the mulled-wine-inspired option combines citrus and spices. It is a thoughtful detail to choose in secret, knowing which fragrances your parents enjoy. The options are listed on the hot tub page.

Dinner chosen with their tastes in mind

For a celebratory evening, you could choose grilled trout with pasta in a creamy champagne sauce. Or our signature barbecue dinner: chicken, halloumi and vegetables with a sour cream, garlic, paprika and herb sauce. The chef takes care of the cooking, leaving your parents to enjoy dinner together.

You can plan a treat for the next morning, too. Breakfast options include syrniki — cottage cheese pancakes — with sour cream and blueberries, crepes filled with cottage cheese, and bread with country butter. Include something they love, the kind of dish you would choose for them at a family breakfast.

The menu will help you choose. Agree the order and serving time before the trip. If your parents prefer cooking over a fire themselves, each chalet has a barbecue area, so a dinner of their favourite dishes can be part of their own plans for the evening.

You arrange the gift; they choose how to spend the day

Once the gift has been presented, the arrangements can remain your responsibility while the weekend belongs to your parents.

They may want a long breakfast and a walk near the chalet. Or they may want to visit Kozelsk, see the whimsical buildings at Vikhlyandia and try a pottery workshop. You know their interests, so you can include a few ideas:

  • What to see in Kozelsk — for people who enjoy small towns and walking.
  • Vikhlyandia park — for unusual places and photographs.
  • Activities at the retreat — for new experiences during the stay.

A small selection like this can extend the surprise. Your parents can look at the photographs and choose what interests them. For ideas for the celebration itself, see “An anniversary at a glamping retreat: ideas for a romantic weekend”.

Care can be part of the gift, too

A trip arranged by their children offers more than a destination. It feels good to know someone has thought about the journey, chosen the cabin and organised dinner.

You could drive your parents there, help with their bags and give them a hug before you leave. If they enjoy travelling in their own car, send them the address, route and booking confirmation.

Put the essentials in the invitation: dates, the chalet you have chosen, the extras you have paid for and a contact number. Your parents will understand what you have arranged, while you keep track of the booking details.

If you can book two nights, they will have a full day between arrival and departure. Time for morning tea, a walk and an evening spent exactly as they wish.

An invitation to their own little journey

Leave space beside their wedding photograph in the card for a new picture from the trip. Add a few words from their children: what you are grateful for, and why you wanted to give them these days together.

Celebrate with the whole family, then let the occasion continue with just the two of them. Wish them a good journey, give them a hug and send them off for a few days you have already taken care of.

Choose a chalet at Na Krayu Zemli for your parents’ wedding anniversary gift. If the dates are still undecided, start by discussing the gift with the team.

Questions about giving your parents a countryside break

Can I give them the trip before choosing dates?

You can present a card inviting your parents to enjoy a stay, then choose the dates together after the celebration. Make the booking separately once you have decided on the dates and the chalet.

How do I book if the children are paying and the parents are staying?

Tell the team that the trip is a gift for your parents. They will explain which guest details they need. You can also agree who should receive the confirmation and who will discuss payment and the extras.

Can the gift include accommodation, a hot tub and meals?

You can order the hot tub, bath preparation and chef-prepared dishes separately alongside the accommodation. Agree the contents of the gift and the total cost in advance, so you know what will be ready for your parents.

What if I want to keep some details secret?

When arranging the trip, discuss which preparations you would like to keep as a surprise. Your parents might know about the chalet but only discover the dinner or bath option during the stay. Agree the possibilities with the team beforehand.

How can I arrange the journey if my parents do not drive?

You can drive them yourself or find public transport to Kozelsk, followed by a taxi to the retreat in Yudinki. The journeys there and back can be part of the gift as well.
