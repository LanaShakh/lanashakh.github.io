It's AI — English blog from scratch

Content manager, SEO copywriter · January 2026 — present

Challenge

Joined as the first copywriter on an AI detection product. The blog and English social channels had to be launched from scratch: no editorial policy, style guide, or templates existed.

What I did

Built the content plan around SEO goals, set up the pipeline from research to Django CMS publication, and wrote the brand standards for article structure, tone, and source work. Topics: AI detection, SEO and Google algorithms, AI in HR and hiring, education. Research comes from primary sources — ManpowerGroup, Edelman, PwC, Deloitte, Google publications and blogs, academic papers. Every text passes an AI detector before publication.

Result

Per SimilarWeb, in March 2026 its-ai.org traffic grew 21% and the X/Twitter audience grew 81%. I've owned the English blog and social channels during this period. Article samples are in the Writing section.

LFCarry — editorial AI workflow

Content manager, prompt engineer · April 2022 — June 2025

Challenge

Joined a gaming services platform as a copywriter and grew into content manager and de facto prompt engineer over three years. The goal: raise publishing speed and quality and bring order to the content process.

What I did

Ran the corporate blog, built service pages with SEO copy and competitor analysis, published in WordPress and a custom CMS with basic HTML. Set up an AI-assisted production workflow: research, structure, draft, hand-finishing. Also wrote prompts for the support and sales AI assistant: analysed errors, rewrote instructions, introduced a response-quality control system.

Result

Organic traffic grew 30% in six months. Copywriting speed increased 7× with no quality loss. AI assistant errors dropped from 46% to 4.8% in two months. After a copywriter team reduction, I stayed on staff and was promoted to content manager.
